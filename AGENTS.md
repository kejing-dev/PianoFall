# Piano Fall (琴键瀑布)

PICO OS 6 (Swan) spatial piano trainer. Kotlin, PICO Spatial SDK 6.1.9, one Mixed Stage (Full Space, passthrough),
package `io.embodit.pianofall`, v0.1.0. Notes fall as light bars down a 0.40 m curtain onto the keys: on your own
piano (two-corner alignment, two world anchors) or on a 1:1 virtual piano put on your desk that really plays
(Salamander piano samples, A4 = 440). Listen / play along (scored) / wait for me; 12 public-domain songs in 4 levels.

Design package: `../spatial-app-factory/apps/piano-fall/design-package.json` (skeleton D, STAGE_MIXED,
single_panel_with_popup; gate B passed 2026-10-27 with the desk piano added). Built from Trace Light
(`../trace-light`: Stage root, panel pool, plane colliders, anchors, controller input) and Spatial Ruler
(controller tip channel, hand input).

On the emulator another app's Full Space blocks the launch ("current full space don't allow start fullscreen"):
force-stop the other factory apps first. The AVD sometimes drops: `pico-cli emulator start --avd
PICO_6_1_Factory2 --adb-device emulator-5562 -y`. Camera down: `tools/harness/emu.sh drag 1080 700 1080 1250
-Button right -NoFast` (the 60 fps gate fails on this machine).

## Debug broadcast (debug build): `tools/dev.sh cmd NAME [TEXT] [NUMBERS]`

`io.embodit.pianofall.DEBUG`, extras `cmd`, `s` (text), `a` (comma numbers). Full list in `DebugCommands.kt`.

- `seedKeyboard "" len,keys,yawDeg` — white-model piano (E8) 0.45 m ahead, 0.40 m below the eyes, aligned, anchors made
- `virtual "" keys` — virtual desk + a placed virtual piano ahead
- `state S00..S24` (S14 / S15 open the overlays, S20 = S08 without MIDI) · `desk` · `nodesk on|off`
- `takeEnd left|right [len]` · `length "" metres` (S04 right end) · `rotate "" deg` · `stick "" x,ms`
- `ghost "" dx,dz,put` (S24, metres from the desk centre; put = 1 places it)
- `song id hand,mode,speed%` (hand 0 L / 1 R / 2 both; mode 0 listen / 1 follow / 2 wait) -> S08
- `noteOn "" midi,holdMs` (MIDI on a real keyboard, a key on the virtual one) · `fingerPress "" midi` · `seek "" sec`
- `fingerNear "" midi,offsetWhites,holdMs` (a virtual-piano finger strike beside a key, through the assist; logs what it resolved to) · `assist easy|standard|precise`
- `handsReady` · `inputDevice NAME|none` · `finish "" perfect,early,missed,wrong` (S13) · `mute`
- `anchorRemoved` · `anchorJump "" mm` · `anchorBack` · `anchorFail on|off`
- `ui NAME` presses a panel control (probe names: havePiano, noPiano, count:88, startAlign, checkC, alignDone, repin,
  level:2, song:twinkle, start, directStart, pause, continue, restart, backBar, library, again, faster, tryFollow,
  fixOctave, toVirtual, settings, closeSettings, inputSource, pianoMenu, moveHere, hidePiano, placeAgain, pianoDone,
  useVirtualDesk, deskUp, deskDown, deskUp5, deskDone, vcount:49, hands:BOTH, mode:WAIT, speed:0.5, lang:ZH, mute)
- Reading back: `dump` · `pose` · `panels` · `perf` · `keyboard`

## Virtual piano assist (v0.1.2)

User on the headset: too many accidental keys, hard to control. `domain/Press.kt`: `AssistLevel` (轻松 default / 标准 / 精确: snap 1 / 0.5 / 0 white widths, strike speed, push depth, debounce 120 / 100 / 80 ms, timing windows 180-350 / 150-300 / 120-250 ms, stray volume, strays counted only on 精确), `KeyAssist.resolve` (snap to the keys `Performance.targets()` wants), `FingerPress` (strike must come from above within 300 ms). `Performance`: chords graded on their first hit, free strays, combo. Mini bar: 连击 N · Perfect / Good. Controller hits buzz via `LocalHandControllerHaptic`. The real piano keeps the design windows. Tests: `KeyAssistTest`.

## Runtime facts

- Scene build waits 1.5 s and steps 150 ms (emulator ANR otherwise, Trace Light). Audio samples load into SoundPool
  at start (~1 s); key -> play() is logged per press (`PIANO t=... latency_ms=`) and as p50 / p95 every 50 presses.
- On-keys panel and mini bar stand behind the curtain (0.22 m), ~0.65 m from a seated player's eyes.
- Desk finding only accepts a horizontal plane 0.25 - 0.75 m below the eyes, ≥ 0.21 m², ahead (±70°) and within 1 m.
- Hand tracking is not started on the emulator (it stopped the gaze + pinch simulation in Spatial Ruler).
- Textured plates use v = 1 at the bitmap top (PlateUv); countdown digits and frame labels go through it.
